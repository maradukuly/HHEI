Sample

We use the first Demographic and Health Survey fielded after each civil war ended: the 2007 Liberia DHS (war ended August 2003) and the 2008 Sierra Leone DHS (war ended January 2002), accessed through IPUMS DHS and linked to DHS GPS cluster coordinates. As an extension, we add the second post-war wave (2013 in both countries), which brings in younger cohorts with partial or no school-age exposure.

To limit bias from war-time displacement, the sample is restricted to permanent residents: respondents who report having always lived in their current place of residence (DHS v104 = “always”). Visitors are excluded. The sample includes women and men aged 15–49 with non-missing literacy.

Indices

\(i\) individual; \(n\) neighborhood (DHS cluster); \(r\) region (county in Liberia, district in Sierra Leone); \(c\) country; \(b\) birth year; \(t\) calendar year; \(w\) survey wave; \(d \in \{d_1, d_2, d_3\}\) distance band; \(k \in \{1,2,3\}\) UCDP violence type (state-based, non-state, one-sided); \(e\) UCDP GED event.

Outcome

\(L_i \in \{0,1\}\) equals 1 if respondent \(i\) reads a whole sentence on the DHS reading card, or completed secondary school or higher, and 0 if they cannot read or read only parts of a sentence. Respondents with no card in a required language or with a visual impairment are set to missing. As a robustness check, partial readers are coded as literate.

Conflict exposure

Distance bands around \(i\)’s neighborhood \(n_i\) are non-overlapping:

Band Distance from \(n_i\)
\(d_1\) 0 – TBD km
\(d_2\) TBD – TBD km
\(d_3\) TBD – TBD km

Events beyond the outer edge of \(d_3\) are excluded.

\(i\)’s school-age window is \(W_i = [b_i + a_0,\; b_i + a_1]\), where \(a_0\) and \(a_1\) are school-entry and school-exit ages (TBD; baseline 6–14).

Each event \(e\) has a year \(t(e)\), violence type \(k(e)\), country \(c(e)\), distance from \(i\)’s neighborhood \(\text{dist}(n_i, e)\), and distance to the Liberia–Sierra Leone border \(\text{db}(e)\). The border zone is defined as \(\text{db}(e) \le B\), with \(B\) TBD. The set of relevant events for \(i\) is

\[\mathcal{R}_{i,d,k} = \{\, e : k(e) = k,\; t(e) \in W_i,\; \text{dist}(n_i, e) \in d \,\}\]

partitioned into three mutually exclusive subsets:

  • Internal conflict exposure: \(\mathcal{R}^{CE}_{i,d,k} = \{ e \in \mathcal{R}_{i,d,k} : c(e) = c_i,\; \text{db}(e) > B \}\)
  • Spillover conflict exposure: \(\mathcal{R}^{SCE}_{i,d,k} = \{ e \in \mathcal{R}_{i,d,k} : c(e) \neq c_i,\; \text{db}(e) > B \}\)
  • Border fighting exposure: \(\mathcal{R}^{BF}_{i,d,k} = \{ e \in \mathcal{R}_{i,d,k} : \text{db}(e) \le B \}\)

The treatment variables are log event counts:

\[CE_{i,d,k} = \ln\!\big(1 + |\mathcal{R}^{CE}_{i,d,k}|\big), \quad SCE_{i,d,k} = \ln\!\big(1 + |\mathcal{R}^{SCE}_{i,d,k}|\big), \quad BF_{i,d,k} = \ln\!\big(1 + |\mathcal{R}^{BF}_{i,d,k}|\big)\]

Specification

\[ L_i = \sum_{d}\sum_{k} \beta_{d,k}\, CE_{i,d,k} + \sum_{d}\sum_{k} \gamma_{d,k}\, SCE_{i,d,k} + \sum_{d}\sum_{k} \theta_{d,k}\, BF_{i,d,k} + X_i'\pi + \mu_{n} + \lambda_{c \times b} + \delta_{w} + \varepsilon_i \]

  • \(X_i\): pre-determined individual controls (sex, ethnicity, religion)
  • \(\mu_n\): neighborhood fixed effects
  • \(\lambda_{c \times b}\): country-by-birth-year fixed effects
  • \(\delta_w\): survey-wave fixed effects (each wave is country-specific)

\(\gamma_{d,k}\) are the parameters of interest: the effect of school-age exposure to conflict of type \(k\) across the border, in band \(d\), conditional on internal exposure and border fighting. The specification is estimated as a weighted linear probability model using DHS sampling weights.

Heterogeneity. Let \(Z_i = 1\) if \(\text{db}(n_i) \le B\), i.e. \(i\) lives in the border zone. We interact \(Z_i\) with \(SCE_{i,d,k}\) and \(BF_{i,d,k}\) to test whether spillover effects differ for border residents. \(Z_i\) itself is absorbed by \(\mu_n\).

Potientially use deaths per 100 if there are too many near zero coef… Also, break coef by affects based on age at time of conflict, multiple betas like: \[L_{igcb} = \beta^{cross}_{early} C^{cross}_{gb,\,6\text{–}10} + \beta^{cross}_{late} C^{cross}_{gb,\,11\text{–}14} + \beta^{own}_{early} C^{own}_{gb,\,6\text{–}10} + \beta^{own}_{late} C^{own}_{gb,\,11\text{–}14} + \ldots\]
